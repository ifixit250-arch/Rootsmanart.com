The Caye Caulker Studio
Biography & Heritage

Armando 'Rootsman' Rivero

The Journey of a Belizean Master of Color and Canvas

Living the Art of the Caribbean

Armando "Rootsman" Rivero is an iconic figure in Belizean contemporary art. Residing on the North side of Caye Caulker, Rootsman embodies the tranquil yet vibrant spirit of island life. He travels between the North and South on his handmade catamaran with his wife, living a simple life with as few material possessions as possible, allowing total dedication to his craft and spiritual connection with the environment.

Rivero moved to Caye Caulker at fifteen, and within a year he was already oil painting, driven by the intense beauty he saw around him. When landmark local signs began to fade, Rootsman stepped in to give them new life, turning public infrastructure into living galleries of marine and cultural wonder.

"They just gave me an idea and I take it from there. So they wanted the underwater team... But then I figured I could combine the whole thing with animals, sharks, and fish."
— Armando 'Rootsman' Rivero

The Island Sign Masterpieces

ROOTSMAN has transformed Caye Caulker's iconic welcome sign and local dive shop facades into monumental outdoor galleries. Featuring cascading sharks, rays, turtles, and cultural musicians, these murals celebrate Belize's rich marine and human heritage.

Artist Statement

Armando 'Rootsman' Rivero

"Living on Caye Caulker gives you a distinct perspective on time and color. When you sail the waters between North and South, you witness hues of turquoise, amber, and gold that you cannot find anywhere else in the world. My canvas is my journal of these waters, these rhythms, and my people."

"The original sign on the island was painted by another well-known local artist, but when it started to fade, Rivero stepped in to give it new life."
